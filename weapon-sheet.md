## Weapon Properties

### Damage Types
- **Slashing** - Effective against unarmored targets
- **Piercing** - Effective against light armor
- **Bludgeoning** - Effective against structures and heavy armor

### Special Properties
- **Versatile** - Can be used one or two-handed
- **Heavy** - Requires STR 13+ to use effectively
- **Two-Handed** - Requires both hands
- **Loading** - Requires action to reload
- **Reach** - 5 ft extra range
- **Thrown** - Can be thrown as ranged attack
- **Concealable** - Easy to hide
- **Improvised** - Not designed as weapon, disadvantage on attacks

### Ammunition Types
- **Pistol/Rifle** - Magazine fed, varying capacities
- **Shotgun** - Shell fed, typically 6-8 rounds
- **Bow/Crossbow** - Arrows/bolts, quiver capacity 20-40
- **Thrown** - Recoverable after combat (usually)

## Weapon Modifications

### Melee Weapon Mods
- **Barbed/Wire** - +1 damage, bleeding effect
- **Weighted Head** - +1 damage, heavy property
- **Extended Reach** - +5 ft reach
- **Grip Wrap** - +1 to hit with STR-based attacks

### Ranged Weapon Mods
- **Scope** - +1 to hit at long range
- **Extended Magazine** - Double ammo capacity
- **Silencer** - Disadvantage on perception checks to hear
- **Laser Sight** - +1 to hit within 30 ft

### Ammunition Types
- **Hollow Point** - +1 damage, disadvantage against armor
- **Armor Piercing** - +2 to hit against armored targets
- **Incendiary** - Fire damage, area effect
- **Explosive** - Area damage, destroys ammo on use

## Weapon Crafting

### Materials Required
- **Scrap Metal** - For melee weapons and parts
- **Wood/Plastic** - For handles and stocks
- **Springs/Mechanisms** - For ranged weapons
- **Adhesives/Fasteners** - For assembly

### Skill Requirements
- **Basic Weapons** - No special skill required
- **Martial Weapons** - Weapon proficiency or training
- **Heavy Weapons** - STR 13+ and proficiency
- **Modified Weapons** - Engineering or mechanics skill

### Crafting Time
- **Simple Melee** - 1-2 hours
- **Martial Melee** - 4-6 hours
- **Basic Ranged** - 6-8 hours
- **Heavy Ranged** - 12-24 hours
- **Modified Weapons** - +50% crafting time

## Weapon Maintenance

### Durability
- **Well-Maintained** - No penalties
- **Worn** - -1 to hit and damage
- **Damaged** - Disadvantage on attacks
- **Broken** - Cannot be used until repaired

### Repair Materials
- **Weapon Parts** - For mechanical repairs
- **Sharpening Tools** - For melee weapons
- **Cleaning Supplies** - For ranged weapons
- **Replacement Parts** - For specific components

### Maintenance Actions
- **Clean & Oil** - Prevents wear (1 hour)
- **Sharpen/Repair** - Fixes worn weapons (2 hours)
- **Full Overhaul** - Fixes damaged weapons (4 hours)
- **Rebuild** - Fixes broken weapons (8 hours)
